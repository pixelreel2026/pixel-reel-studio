# Pixel & Reel Studio - Design & Content Guide

## Visual Design System

### Color Palette (Dark Luxury Theme)
- **Primary Background**: #0A0A0A (Deep Black)
- **Secondary Background**: #1A1A1A (Dark Gray)
- **Accent Color**: #00D9FF (Cyan/Electric Blue)
- **Secondary Accent**: #FF006E (Vibrant Pink)
- **Text Primary**: #FFFFFF (White)
- **Text Secondary**: #B0B0B0 (Light Gray)
- **Border Color**: #2A2A2A (Subtle Dark Border)

### Typography
- **Font Family**: Inter (Arabic subset included)
- **Headings**: Bold, sizes 32px-48px
- **Body Text**: Regular, 16px
- **Captions**: Light, 14px

### Effects & Animations
- Smooth fade-ins on scroll
- Hover effects with subtle scale (1.05)
- Gradient overlays for depth
- Glassmorphism effects for cards
- Smooth transitions (300ms ease-out)

## Animation Guidelines

- Section entrance: fade-in + slide-up (500ms)
- Card hover: scale 1.05 + shadow increase (300ms)
- Button click: scale 0.97 + color shift (150ms)
- Scroll animations: trigger at 80% viewport visibility
- Stagger effect: 50ms delay between items
